Predict Whist

A scorecard for the card table. Three to six players, 13 rounds, no account.

Support

Open a support request or view existing requests. The form requires a GitHub account. Requests and replies are public. Include your app version, iPhone model and a short description. Do not include player names, saved games, contact details, passwords or other private information.

Where are my games?

Completed games are in the options menu under Saved games. Paused games appear in player setup. Entries are saved on the device after each change.

Will my history move to another phone?

The app has no account or automatic sync. Your iOS backup settings may include its saved data. Removing the app can remove its local history.

Why are some numbers grey?

Grey numbers are not valid choices under the current rules. During results entry, the remaining tricks limit the choices. The last player must enter the exact number left. During bidding, the optional rule prevents total bids from matching the card count.

How do I correct a round?

Use the undo arrow in the top bar. Redo restores the undone round until you make another entry. Use Previous player or Edit to correct an entry before confirming.

Why does the display stay on?

The app is designed to stay on the table for players to read and use. Normal screen locking returns when you leave the app. You can still lock the phone with its side button.

Does this app use the old web account?

No. The iOS app does not connect to the old web service. Any records on that service remain separate.
