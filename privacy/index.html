Predict Whist iOS privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026. This policy applies to the local-only Predict Whist iOS app published by Adam Williams.

Your game data

Player names, settings, bids, tricks, scores and game history are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not send this information to us or to a game server. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking or analytics services.

Sharing and backups

When you choose Share, iOS lets you select a destination for the results. The destination you choose handles that copy under its own policies. Your device and iOS backup settings may include the app’s stored data. Apple manages those services; we cannot access your personal device backup.

Keeping and removing games

History stays on your device until it is removed. Removing the app can remove its local data. Restoring a device backup may restore a saved copy. There is no automatic account sync or server recovery.

Earlier versions and the web service

An update from a development version can copy guest games and the currently signed-in account’s cached games into the local store. It does not download records from the old web service. Existing web-service accounts and records are separate from this iOS app and are not erased by this update.

Support

If you email info@whistscorer.com, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information. You can contact the same address about a support message or this policy.

Apple and this website

Apple operates the App Store, iOS and any device diagnostics or backup services you choose to use under its own terms and privacy policy. This support website is hosted by GitHub, which may process normal website request logs under its own privacy policy. The website has no added analytics or advertising scripts.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
